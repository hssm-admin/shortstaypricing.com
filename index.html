Rental revenue means the nightly charges for nights stayed, before platform commissions, excluding cleaning fees and taxes.

The free RevPAR check

What is your home earning per available night?

RevPAR is what your home earns per night it's available, booked or not. It's the fairest single number for pricing. Get yours one of two ways.

Easiest

Send us your listing link

No numbers needed. We compare your home with similar homes nearby and email you a one-page check within two business days. It's free, with no obligation.

Or work it out yourself now

Where to find your numbers: open your Airbnb earnings page (in the Airbnb app: Menu, then Earnings) and use the arrow to go back to last year. It shows your gross earnings, and your nights booked under Performance stats. Not on Airbnb? Your booking software's reports have the same figures.

Questions

The things hosts ask first.

What do I need for the free check?

Just your listing link and your email. We do the numbers and email you a one-page check within two business days. If you'd rather see your figures straight away, the calculator needs two numbers from your Airbnb earnings page.

Do I need PriceLabs?

Yes. We're a PriceLabs Verified RM Partner and work inside your own PriceLabs account. If you're not on it yet, start their free 30-day trial and connect Airbnb or your booking software. We'll send you a short step-by-step guide, and if you'd like a hand, we'll walk you through it on a quick call. You do the clicks, so you never share a password with us.

Will you touch my guests or bookings?

No. We only manage your pricing. Your guests, bookings, payouts and listing stay with you.

Can I set a lowest price?

Yes. You set a minimum nightly price for each listing and we never go below it. Change it any time by email.

How do you charge?

5% of each listing's rental revenue for the month, or $99 if that comes to less, plus GST. We charge once a month, in arrears. You get a statement showing each listing's nights, revenue and fee, then it's charged to the card you saved when you signed up.

What if I want to stop?

Give us 30 days' notice by email. There's no exit fee. We remove our access and leave your settings as they are.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Your market, your listing and your reviews all matter. What we commit to is the work: your pricing reviewed every week, adjusted as your market moves, and reported every month.

Where do you work?

Anywhere in Australia, with more countries to come.

How is this connected to Hobart Short Stay Management?

Same team. Hobart Short Stay Management runs homes for owners from start to finish; Short Stay Pricing does the pricing only, for hosts who run their own. Your details stay with Short Stay Pricing unless you ask to hear about full management.

Can I talk to someone first?

Of course. Email hello@shortstaypricing.com and we'll set up a 15-minute call. Most hosts don't need one, but it's there.
